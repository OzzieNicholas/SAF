\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\zihao{-5}
\noindent\textbf{摘要：}
金属增材制造中，下朝悬垂面并不必然是真实支撑--零件界面，但可作为可复核的潜在支撑接触区代理。本文在固定构建方向、工具半径和离散方向集下定义有限球工具几何可达性代理，并采用形状条件神经网络预测未见零件上的该属性。
若存在一个离散逼近方向，使半径为 $r$ 的球工具可沿直线无碰撞到达代理查询点，则该点可达。
标签生成由体素距离场筛选与边界带连续胶囊--网格距离细化组成，并通过差分、跨分辨率和原始网格质量审计披露数值与数据边界。
以 8,590 个公开 CAD 零件为主数据源，网络分别输出点级不可达概率和 64 个固定方向的被挡概率；网络不接收工具半径、构建方向或方向向量作为条件。
结果表明，Fusion 与程序化数据中分别有 4.86\% 和 29.25\% 的接触点射线可见但有限工具不可达；五次重复模型级随机留出（每次按零件 60/20/20 切分）得到零件等权 F1 $0.793\pm0.022$、IoU $0.737\pm0.025$，每次零件 F1 的中位数均值和 IQR 均值分别为 $0.948\pm0.029$ 与 $0.313\pm0.032$。
在固定 80 件留出集上，shape-conditioned surrogate 的点池 F1 为 0.920，高于零半径射线的 0.676 和 16 全局方向粗有限工具 proxy 的 0.853；相对射线的逐件配对差为 0.459，95\% 置信区间为 $\lbrack 0.372,0.545\rbrack$。方向级逐方向 F1 为 $0.901\pm0.004$，Top-1 推荐命中率为 0.959。
该结果是仅考虑零件本体遮挡的固定代理配置 surrogate fidelity，不构成真实支撑接触或后处理认证，而是为面向增材制造的设计提供区别于支撑量的风险观察维度。

\noindent\textbf{关键词：}增材制造；支撑结构；工具可达性；面向增材制造的设计；深度学习；几何代理评价

\vspace{0.45em}
\noindent\textbf{Abstract:}
In metal additive manufacturing, a downward-facing surface is not necessarily a realized support--part interface, but it can serve as a reproducible proxy for a potential support-contact region.
We define a finite-spherical-tool accessibility proxy under a fixed build direction, tool radius, and discrete approach set, and predict it on unseen parts with a shape-conditioned neural network.
A point is accessible if a spherical tool of radius $r$ reaches the proxy query point along at least one collision-free discrete straight approach.
Label generation combines voxel distance-field screening with continuous capsule--mesh distance refinement in a boundary band; differential, cross-resolution, and raw-mesh-quality audits disclose the numerical and data boundaries.
On a materialized corpus of 8,590 public CAD parts, contact amount is only weakly associated with inaccessibility.
In the Fusion and procedural sets, 4.86\% and 29.25\% of contacts, respectively, are ray-visible but unreachable by the finite tool.
Five repeated model-level holdouts (60/20/20 part splits, with possible test-set overlap) yield equal-part inaccessible-class F1 $0.793\pm0.022$ and IoU $0.737\pm0.025$; the mean per-repeat median F1 and IQR are $0.948\pm0.029$ and $0.313\pm0.032$.
On a fixed 80-part holdout, the shape-conditioned surrogate has pooled F1 0.920, compared with 0.676 for zero-radius ray visibility and 0.853 for a 16-global-direction coarse finite-tool proxy; the paired difference over ray visibility is 0.459 with a 95\% confidence interval of $\lbrack 0.372,0.545\rbrack$. The directional field reaches per-direction F1 $0.901\pm0.004$ and a Top-1 recommendation hit rate of 0.959.
These results establish surrogate fidelity for the stated frozen proxy under part-body occlusion only, not physical post-processing feasibility or a continuous toolpath.

\noindent\textbf{Key words:} additive manufacturing; support structures; tool accessibility; design for additive manufacturing; deep learning; geometric proxy evaluation
